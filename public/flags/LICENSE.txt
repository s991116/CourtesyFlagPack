Local flags that are not part of flag-icons are from Wikimedia Commons.
Each of these files keeps the license named here.

- bq.svg — Flag of Bonaire. Public domain.
- bq-sa.svg — Flag of Saba. CC BY-SA 3.0.
- bq-se.svg — Flag of Sint Eustatius. CC BY-SA 4.0. Drawn by User:Andrwsc.
- bl.svg — Flag of Saint Barthélemy (local). CC BY 2.5. The arms are by User:Manassas.
- gf.svg — Flag of French Guiana. Public domain. Drawn by Madden.
- gp.svg — Unofficial flag of Guadeloupe (local). Public domain. Drawing by Pierre Gay.
- mf.svg — Local flag of the Collectivity of Saint Martin. Licence Ouverte. Government of the Collectivity of Saint-Martin, vectorised by Flagvisioner.
- pm.svg — Flag of Saint-Pierre and Miquelon. Public domain.
- re.svg — Proposed flag of Réunion (VAR). Public domain.
- wf.svg — Flag of Wallis and Futuna. Public domain.
- yt.svg — Flag of Mayotte (local). Public domain.
- pt-20.svg — Flag of the Azores. Public domain, from the Xrmap flag collection.
- pt-30.svg — Flag of Madeira. Drawn for this project.
- gb-shet.svg — Flag of Shetland. Drawn for this project.
- vg.svg — British Virgin Islands civil ensign. The flag-icons blue ensign with the field changed to red, for the courtesy flag.
- us.svg — Flag of the United States. Public domain. The geometric Wikimedia flag, so the union keeps its official share of the 10:19 flag.
- q.svg — Plain yellow quarantine flag (maritime signal flag Q). Drawn for this project.
- dive.svg — Red diver-down flag with a white diagonal stripe. Drawn for this project.
- distress.svg — Orange distress signal with a black square and circle. Drawn for this project.
- sig-a.svg through sig-z.svg — International Code signal flags A to Z. Drawn for this project.
